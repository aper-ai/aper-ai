## 🔬 Our mission | 我们的使命

Aper AI develops evidence-based approaches to research integrity. We aim to help researchers, journals, and institutions identify potential concerns, examine supporting evidence, and make informed decisions with appropriate human review.

我们关注科研成果的真实性与可核验性，通过技术工具与专业复核，为科研人员、期刊及机构提供更清晰的证据线索。

## 🧭 Areas of focus | 关注领域

| | Focus | What we explore |
|:---:|:---|:---|
| 🖼️ | **Scientific image integrity** | Screening figures for potential duplication and other image-related concerns. |
| 🧬 | **Research data verification** | Examining data and source materials for inconsistencies that warrant review. |
| 📚 | **Scholarly review** | Organizing publication information and contextual evidence for assessment. |
| 🔎 | **Evidence workflows** | Making findings easier to inspect, document, and discuss. |

## ⚖️ Our principles | 工作原则

- **Evidence first** — Present clear, reviewable information and preserve context.
- **Human judgment** — Screening results are leads for assessment, not conclusions of misconduct.
- **Responsible communication** — Distinguish observations, uncertainty, and interpretation.
- **Respect for research** — Support fair, careful evaluation and constructive scientific dialogue.

> 科研诚信核验需要证据、上下文与专业判断。工具发现的线索应经过人工复核，不能单独作为认定学术不端的依据。
